The Person Behind the Professional

Beyond the Resume

❤️ Why I'm in Recruitment

The pandemic changed the way I looked at work.

During COVID-19, I witnessed people desperately searching for oxygen cylinders, blood donors, medicines, and hospital beds. Rather than watching from the sidelines, I brought together hundreds of strangers into a volunteer network so they could help one another find critical resources when every minute mattered.

That experience changed my perspective. I realised that behind every request for help was a person trying to protect their family, preserve their dignity, or simply survive.

When the pandemic subsided, I asked myself how I could continue making a meaningful difference through my career. I found the answer in recruitment.

To me, recruitment is not just about filling vacancies or achieving hiring targets. It is about connecting people with opportunities that allow them to earn a living, support their families, and build a future with dignity.

Employment gives people more than a salary — it provides confidence, purpose, and independence. While I know recruitment alone cannot solve every social challenge, I believe that helping even one person find meaningful work can positively affect their life, their family, and their community.

That belief continues to shape the way I approach every conversation, every candidate, and every client relationship.
